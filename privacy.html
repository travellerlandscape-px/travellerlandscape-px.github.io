Privacy Policy

px-media is a private application used exclusively by the owner of the YouTube channel "P-X" to upload, schedule and analyse videos on his own channel.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, px-media uses Google APIs (YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics API, Google Drive API) to read the owner's own video files from Google Drive, upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel and read the channel's statistics.

Use and sharing

Data is used only for these purposes. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. No data of other users is collected.

Storage and deletion

Access tokens are stored encrypted and can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

px-media's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
